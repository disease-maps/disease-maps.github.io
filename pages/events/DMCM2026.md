# <u>10th Disease Maps Community Meeting</u>

**5–7 October 2026 · University of Rostock, Germany**

The 10th Disease Maps Community Meeting is organized by the [Department of Systems Biology and Bioinformatics](https://www.sbi.uni-rostock.de/) at the University of Rostock, Germany.

## <u>Venue</u>

The conference takes place in the historic Main Building of the University of Rostock, in the heart of the city center. The building is more than 150 years old, and the university, founded in 1419, is one of the oldest universities in Northern Europe.

### <u>Session 1: Computational Models of Disease Processes</u>

**Keynote speaker**

<a href="https://www.uni.lu/lcsb-en/people/marek-ostaszewski/" target="_blank">
  <img src="/images/people/MarekOstaszewski1.jpg" alt="Marek Ostaszewski" width="135"/>
</a>

**[Marek Ostaszewski](https://www.uni.lu/lcsb-en/people/marek-ostaszewski/)**  
Luxembourg Centre for Systems Biomedicine (LCSB), University of Luxembourg

**Keynote:** *Disease Maps Community: bridging knowledge and models with standards, software, and suffering*

Marek Ostaszewski is a senior researcher at the Luxembourg Centre for Systems Biomedicine, University of Luxembourg. His work focuses on systems-biology approaches to human disease, molecular network construction and analysis, visual exploration of biomedical knowledge and data, and translational applications in neurodegenerative and chronic immune disorders.

---

### <u>Session 2: Applications for AI in Model Curation, Exploration, and Analysis</u>

**Keynote speaker · remote**

<a href="https://slobentanzer.github.io/" target="_blank">
  <img src="/images/people/sebastianlobentanzer.jpg" alt="Sebastian Lobentanzer" width="135"/>
</a>

**[Sebastian Lobentanzer](https://slobentanzer.github.io/)**  
Helmholtz Munich, Germany

**Keynote:** *Powerful ≠ Ready: AI and knowledge representation in biomedicine*

Dr. Sebastian Lobentanzer is a Principal Investigator at Helmholtz Munich, a Helmholtz AI Young Investigator, and head of the Computational Biology Unit at the German Center for Diabetes Research. His research focuses on reliable automation of scientific work, biomedical knowledge graphs, large language models, agentic AI, and the governance and evaluation of AI systems for science.

---

### <u>Session 3: Recent Advances in Systems Biology</u>

**Keynote speaker**

<a href="https://www.sbi.uni-rostock.de/team/detail/prof-olaf-wolkenhauer" target="_blank">
  <img src="/images/people/OlafWolkenhauer.jpg" alt="Olaf Wolkenhauer" width="135"/>
</a>

**[Olaf Wolkenhauer](https://www.sbi.uni-rostock.de/team/detail/prof-olaf-wolkenhauer)**  
University of Rostock, Germany

**Keynote:** *Disease Maps - Repositories or models?*

Olaf Wolkenhauer is Professor at the University of Rostock. His research focuses on systems biology and on uncertainty in the analysis and interpretation of life-science data, with particular emphasis on mathematical and computational approaches to biological and medical systems.

---

### <u>Session 4: Systems Medicine and Clinical Translation</u>

**Keynote speaker**

<a href="https://tu-dresden.de/med/mf/imb/das-institut/beschaeftigte/persoenliche-profile-der-mitarbeitenden/markus-wolfien" target="_blank">
  <img src="/images/people/wolfien-e5f0e109.jpg" alt="Markus Wolfien" width="135"/>
</a>

**[Markus Wolfien](https://tu-dresden.de/med/mf/imb/das-institut/beschaeftigte/persoenliche-profile-der-mitarbeitenden/markus-wolfien)**  
Institute for Medical Informatics and Biometry, TUD Dresden University of Technology, Germany

**Keynote:** *From Maps to Decisions - Disease Maps as Mechanistic Infrastructure for Precision Medicine*

Dr.-Ing. Markus Wolfien is Group Lead for Data Science & Bioinformatics and Deputy Head of the Chair for Medical Informatics at the Institute for Medical Informatics and Biometry, TU Dresden. His work spans translational systems medicine, biomedical data science, multi-omics, AI-based methods, interoperable clinical data infrastructures, and mechanistic disease modeling for precision medicine and clinical decision support.
